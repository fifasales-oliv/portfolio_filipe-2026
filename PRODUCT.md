# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Potential clients and employers evaluating Filipe's brand design and brand strategy work to decide whether to hire or contract him. They're scanning quickly, comparing against other portfolios, and looking for evidence of craft, taste, and strategic thinking — not just pretty pictures. They want to walk away convinced he can think AND execute, and know how to reach him.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio showcasing brand design and brand strategy work. It exists to win client/employer trust and convert that trust into contact. Success looks like: a visitor remembers the site after closing the tab, understands what Filipe does and how he thinks, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality
Playful & expressive, but disciplined — personality and motion carry the voice, not chaos. Reference: mindfvkness.com ("simple yet powerful with animations") — restrained surface, expressive motion, confident pacing. The site should feel like a brand strategist's own brand: intentional, distinctive, never decorative-for-its-own-sake.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS/template portfolio look: cream/beige template backgrounds, stock "creative agency" clichés, identical card grids, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows, numbered section markers as default scaffolding, gradient text, side-stripe borders. Nothing that reads as a buttoned-up corporate agency site or an unedited template.

## Design Principles
- Show, don't tell: case studies and craft details do the persuading, not adjectives.
- Motion as voice: animation is part of the brand statement (per mindfvkness.com), not an afterthought — purposeful, confident easing, never gratuitous.
- Restraint amplifies expression: a simple surface makes the expressive moments land harder.
- Practice what you preach: a brand strategist's own site is the strongest case study he has.
- Every interaction should leave a clear next step toward contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Good baseline practice: solid color contrast, keyboard-navigable, semantic structure. No strict WCAG compliance target requested. Reduced-motion alternatives still expected per the skill's motion guidance.
